This chart has been developed by the TripSit.me team and is based on the best available scientific research and experience reports. It is intended to serve as a quick reference guide, and further research is always recommended, especially for individuals with medical conditions, taking medications, or with related predispositions. When mixing substances, it is important to remember that many of them can have additive effects and it is best to start with lower doses. Interactions for DXM, DPH, and pregabalin are presented in the context of recreational use, and some interactions may differ when taken at therapeutic doses.
